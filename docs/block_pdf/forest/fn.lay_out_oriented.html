pub fn lay_out_oriented(arena: Arena, root: NodeId, vertically: bool) -> Arena
Expand description

lay_out, with the axes the caller’s to choose: depth runs left to right, or down the page when vertically is set.

The choice matters once a drawing is a page rather than a window: a chain is deep and narrow and reads left to right, while a hub’s fan is one level deep and thousands of siblings broad, and drawn horizontally it is a ribbon a few points wide. Turned on its side it is a figure.
