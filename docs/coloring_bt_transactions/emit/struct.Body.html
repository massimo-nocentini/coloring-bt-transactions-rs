Expand description

What follows the transaction id and the tab on a line.

One definition of it, driven either straight off the store on the fold thread or off a Snapshot on a worker, so the serial and threaded paths cannot drift into writing different bytes.

Fields§

§line: &'a mut Vec<u8>§form: Line§weighted: bool§written: bool

Whether a term has been written, which is what puts the comma between terms rather than after each of them. for_each_term does not say which term is last, so it is this rather than a truncation at the end.

§mass: f64

The four running sums both of the collapsed forms are built from, in one pass over the terms and no memory at all: sum w, sum w.b, sum w.b^2, sum w^2.

Line::Sum prints the second on its own; Line::Moments turns all four into a mean, a spread and an effective count. Kept here rather than in the driver’s loop so the serial and threaded paths accumulate them the same way, as they format the terms the same way.

§first: f64§second: f64§squares: f64§exact: bool

Set when the store handed over the first two moments outright, so term must not add to them a second time.

§laplace: [f64; 7]

sum w . exp(-s . b / L) at each s of crate::bands::LAPLACE, which is all Line::Laplace prints. Summed here off an exact colour’s terms, or handed over whole by a store that carries them, in which case the terms are not walked at all – the way a handed-over Line::Sum is not.

§scale: f64

Where an exponent sits on the block axis, as crate::store::ColorStore::placement gives it: the identity for the stores whose exponents are block ids, and the band geometry for the one whose exponents are bands. Only the collapsed forms read it.

§offset: f64§narrow: bool

Whether a coefficient is an f32 riding in an f64, and prints as one – crate::store::ColorStore::NARROW.

pub fn from_exact_moments(self, moments: [f64; 3]) -> Self

Take the first two moments from the store instead of summing them off the terms, and say so.

For a store whose terms are blocks the two agree, so nothing calls this. For crate::bands they do not: summing its terms gives the centre of a band where the block was wanted, out by up to half a band, and these are exact. The terms are then not walked at all for Line::Moments, which is also why a binned moments run is faster than a binned terms one.

Source

pub fn from_exact_laplace(self, values: [f64; 7]) -> Self

Take the seven Line::Laplace values from the store instead of summing them off the terms. The same hand-over as Body::from_exact_moments, for the same reason: a band store’s terms are bands, and the sum off them would put each block at its band’s centre. The caller does not walk the terms after this, so term has nothing to skip.

fn moments(&self) -> (f64, f64, f64)

The mean block id, the spread about it, and the effective number of blocks – what Line::Moments prints, from the running sums.

A colour with no terms answers three zeroes rather than three NaNs: it is what Line::Sum already prints for one, and a NaN in a column is worse than a zero for every reader of it.
