Positions an input is read for: the amount at 1 and the previous tx id at 2.

The amount is only wanted by --weighted, but it is read either way. Making the mask depend on the mode would push a branch into the innermost parsing loop to save one field of four on runs that do not use it, and the measurement that matters here is that reading a field costs a digit_run and eight-digit folding, not a per-field decision.
