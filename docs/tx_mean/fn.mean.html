Function mean 

Source 
pub(crate) fn mean(store: &WeightedSets, color: &Rc<Terms>) -> f64
Expand description

sum_b b . weight(b), the block id a colour sits at on average.

Divided by the weights rather than trusting them to sum to 1. They do sum to 1 – that is the invariant --stats reports drift against – but the division is one instruction per record against a walk of the whole colour, and it makes the answer a weighted mean by construction instead of by argument.

A colour with no terms cannot come out of the fold above: every colour starts as a single block and a union never empties one. Answering 0 rather than a NaN is what to do with a case that does not arise.
